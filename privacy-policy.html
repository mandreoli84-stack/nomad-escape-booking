2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may process your name, contact details (such as email address and WhatsApp number), booking details, chosen experience, date, number of passengers, pickup information, payment and booking status, and communications you send us. Technical information necessary to operate and secure the booking process may also be processed.

3. Why we use your data and legal bases

  • Bookings and customer support: to manage reservations, coordinate pickups, send booking information and respond to questions, where necessary to perform a contract or take steps at your request before entering one.
  • Payments and records: to process payment-related information and comply with applicable accounting and tax obligations, based on contract performance and legal obligations as applicable.
  • Security and abuse prevention: to protect the booking service and investigate misuse, where supported by our legitimate interests and applicable law.
  • Marketing: only where we have an appropriate legal basis, such as valid consent where required. You may withdraw consent at any time.

4. Payments

Online deposits may be processed through the Getnet/Santander payment service. Payment-card details entered on the payment provider's secure pages are handled by that provider under its own applicable privacy and security terms. We may receive payment references, amounts and transaction statuses needed to administer bookings and reconcile payments.

5. Service providers and partners

Personal information may be shared, only as necessary, with providers supporting website hosting, booking infrastructure, payment processing, communications and operational fulfilment. Where a reservation is made through a business partner, relevant booking information may be exchanged with that partner to provide the booked experience. We do not sell your personal information.

6. International transfers

Some technology or communications providers may process information outside the European Economic Area. Where international transfers occur, we will use an applicable lawful transfer mechanism and any additional safeguards required by data protection law.

7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described here, including providing the experience, resolving issues, maintaining required financial records and meeting legal obligations. Different records may have different retention periods depending on their purpose and applicable law.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request access to, rectification or erasure of your personal data, restriction of processing, data portability, or object to processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

To exercise your rights, email info@nomadescapefuerteventura.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

9. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use storage or similar technologies necessary for essential functionality, such as maintaining a booking session and protecting the service. Any non-essential cookies or tracking technologies must be disclosed and, where legally required, activated only after obtaining appropriate consent. Further cookie details should be provided if such technologies are used.

10. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services or legal obligations. The current version will be published on this page.
